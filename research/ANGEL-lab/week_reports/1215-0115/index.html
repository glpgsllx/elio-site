    Prompt 调整（新增规则）¶

    Text Only
    Act as a Clinical Logic Parser. Extract specific CLINICAL NODES from the text to build a decision tree.

CRITICAL RULE: ATOMICITY (Split Conditions from Actions)
- BAD Node: "24 months recall interval for patients aged 18+" (Do NOT do this)
- GOOD Nodes: "Patients aged 18+" (Node 1) AND "Recall interval: 24 months" (Node 2)

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Do NOT extract generic nouns (e.g., 'table', 'data', 'committee').
2. Extract ATOMIC CONDITIONS: specific symptoms, measurements, or states (e.g., 'Age > 60', 'SBP >= 140mmHg').
3. Extract ATOMIC ACTIONS: interventions or recommendations WITHOUT the condition attached (e.g., 'Offer ABPM', 'Recall interval: 3 months').
4. Extract POPULATIONS: target groups as separate nodes (e.g., 'Adults', 'Children', 'Patients with type 2 diabetes').
5. AVOID CONNECTORS: If a phrase contains words like "for", "if", "when", "due to", SPLIT it into separate nodes.
6. The output must be a list of precise, standalone string phrases.

    Problem¶

    • 跨多学科（牙科、避孕、精神健康、营养、粪便失禁等）合并后，实体/关系中混入大量与当前问题无关的节点（噪声大）。
    • 回答“复诊间隔”时，检索结果出现 3/6/12/21/24 个月等多值，缺少上下文过滤与排序，易产生“答非所问”的混杂输出。尽管可以使用LLM根据查询含义进行选择（最久的时间）
    • 图谱规则（LEADS_TO / EXCEPT_IF / OR 等）混合，且不同文档的表达不一致，语义泛化导致节点关联被过度合并或误合并。
    • 文档长度差异较大（7k~70k 字符），长文解析易丢失位置/章节信息，短文则信息不足，导致权重分配不均。
    • 缺少“主题域过滤”或“按疾病/场景聚类”的前置步骤，导致跨领域检索把无关节点拉入 top 结果。
